# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in BioResearch AI a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or expertise.

We pledge to foster an open, collaborative environment where scientific curiosity, constructive discussion, and respectful communication are valued.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and professional
* Welcoming newcomers
* Providing constructive feedback
* Accepting differing viewpoints
* Focusing on evidence and technical discussion
* Helping improve documentation and code quality
* Acting with empathy and integrity

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or personal attacks
* Discrimination of any kind
* Trolling or inflammatory comments
* Publishing private information without permission
* Intentionally disruptive behavior
* Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional in a collaborative open-source community

---

# Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* GitHub issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Project documentation
* Community spaces
* Any official project communication channels

---

# Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled respectfully and confidentially whenever possible.

---

# Enforcement

Maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

* Warning the contributor
* Requesting changes
* Removing content
* Temporarily restricting participation
* Permanently banning repeated or severe offenders

The response will depend on the severity and frequency of the behavior.
